Travel Journal

Kyoto — May 2022

My 4th city in my solo travel to Japan. I spent hours following locals around Nishiki Market.

It was hot, and I remember my shoes being too tight.

I couldn't help but remember how I would only get hand-me-downs from my sisters, and they never fit properly.

Perpetuating my own discomfort even in adulthood.

Always buying and collecting things, making me uncomfortable even when given the option not to.

Maybe we are all just creatures of habit.
