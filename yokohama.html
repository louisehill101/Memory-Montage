Yokohama — May 2022

I had never heard of this city before — I randomly got lost and landed here.

Usually I would be scared, but I was solo travelling anyway.

I trusted my gut and got off.

I walked around and found the prettiest city ever.

Maybe a testament to trusting your gut, or maybe just a photo of a nice garden.

I really value this memory.
