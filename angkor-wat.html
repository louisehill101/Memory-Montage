Travel Journal

Angkor Wat, Siem Reap — June 2022

I've been so many times — every time I come, I notice something different.

I showed the guard my passport, and he saw I was born in Cambodia. He let me in for free, no questions.

My uncle and cousins were with me — very awkward people, we maybe shared two conversations.

But everyone really cares for each other without thinking.

I'm not even close to these people, but they've done so much for me.
