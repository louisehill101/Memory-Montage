Travel Journal

Phnom Penh — June 2022

I was waiting in the car after my aunty left to get money for something she'd made.

It was hot, and the city looked so vibrant.

It might have been 36 degrees, but I felt warm inside from being around my family.

She always found a way to treat me, no matter how much money she had.

My favourite family member.
