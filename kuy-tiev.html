Travel Journal

Cambodia — July 2022

I was hanging out with my aunty and baby cousin, Momo — she's so cute.

We didn't have much to do, but my aunty always wanted me to feel good.

We all got into her car and drove for about two hours, and landed at a random Khmer wedding.

Khmer people are so welcoming — to me and my family, they just invited us in and cooked for us.

To this day it's still the best BBQ I've ever had.

It made me realise I love Cambodia more and more.
